# Fleet photo edits

Mode: built-in image editor. Edited assets are saved beside their originals under `assets/fleet/`, with `-white-edited.png` filenames. Active paths are listed in `fleet-manifest.json`. Original photographs are retained.

Scope: fleet vehicle cards and galleries only. Service banners, the “01 / Airport Transfers” section, and subsequent sections are unchanged.

Prompt set:
- Exteriors: isolate the original vehicle on pure white seamless studio surroundings with a subtle contact shadow. Preserve vehicle model, viewpoint, proportions, trim and branding; remove garage or outdoor surroundings, glare and environmental reflections. Keep the whole vehicle visible.
- Interiors: preserve the original cabin, seats, materials, layout, equipment and viewpoint. Replace only exterior scenery through windows or open doors with white daylight. Balance exposure and remove glare without redesigning the cabin.
- Enclosed amenities: preserve surfaces and fixtures, balance exposure and lighting; do not invent windows or exterior backgrounds.
